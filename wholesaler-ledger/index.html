Your records, your choice

Local first. Cloud backup only when you choose it.

Core ledger information is stored on the user’s device. Google Drive access is optional and is used only for app backup and restore after the user connects an account.

App-specific Google Drive access

The app uses its private application-data folder and does not access ordinary Google Drive documents.

User-controlled backup

The user chooses when to connect, create a backup, restore data, or disconnect the Google Account.

Simple privacy information

The privacy policy clearly explains what the app stores and why Google access is requested.
