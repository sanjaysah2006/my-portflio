Privacy Policy

In short: Wholesaler Ledger stores business ledger information primarily on the user’s device. Google Drive backup is optional and uses app-specific Drive storage only for backup and restore.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Wholesaler Ledger accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when a user uses the Android application and its optional Google Drive backup feature.

Wholesaler Ledger is developed by Sanjay Kumar Sah. By using the app, the user acknowledges the practices described in this policy.

2. Information handled by the app

Depending on the features used, the app may store:

  • Wholesaler names, mobile numbers, and contact details entered by the user.
  • Bill amounts, payment amounts, transaction dates, payment methods, notes, and outstanding balances.
  • Receipt, bill, PDF, image, or other attachment files selected by the user.
  • App settings, preferences, exported files, and backup information.
  • Limited Google Account information provided during sign-in, such as the connected email address, to identify the account used for backup.

The app does not require users to enter information beyond what is needed for the features they choose to use.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

Connecting a Google Account is optional. If the user enables Google Drive backup, Wholesaler Ledger requests the following permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This permission allows the app to use its own hidden application-data folder in the user’s Google Drive. It is used only to:

  • Create application backups requested or enabled by the user.
  • Find backups previously created by Wholesaler Ledger.
  • Restore application data when the user requests a restore.
  • Replace or manage backup files created by the app.

Wholesaler Ledger does not use this permission to access the user’s ordinary Google Drive documents, photos, or unrelated files.

4. How information is used

Information handled by the app is used to provide functions requested by the user, including:

  • Creating and displaying wholesaler accounts and ledger entries.
  • Calculating bills, payments, and outstanding balances.
  • Attaching supporting receipts or files.
  • Creating, exporting, backing up, and restoring ledger data.
  • Maintaining app preferences and responding to support requests.

Google user data is used only to provide and maintain the Google Drive backup and restore feature described in this policy.

5. Sharing and transfer

Wholesaler Ledger does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling.

Information may be shared only when the user deliberately exports or shares it, when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local ledger information remains on the user’s device until the user deletes records, clears the app’s data, or uninstalls the app. Exported files remain wherever the user saves or shares them.

Google Drive backups remain associated with the connected Google Account until they are replaced or removed through available app or Google Account controls, or the Google Account is deleted.

Users may request assistance with data deletion by emailing sanjaysha2412@gmail.com. Users should not send passwords or confidential ledger contents by email.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Users are responsible for protecting their device, screen lock, Google Account, and exported backup files.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. User choices and controls

Users can choose whether to enter optional details, attach files, export records, or connect a Google Account. Users can also:

  • Edit or delete ledger entries within the app.
  • Disconnect the Google Account from the backup screen.
  • Revoke Wholesaler Ledger’s access through Google Account controls.
  • Clear local app data or uninstall the app.
  • Contact the developer for privacy or deletion assistance.

9. Children’s privacy

Wholesaler Ledger is a business record-keeping application and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly request children’s personal information through the app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, legal obligations, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
