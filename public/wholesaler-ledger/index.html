Latest app update · 11 September 2026

Safer backups and dependable offline access.

The latest release improves account access, trial visibility, and data recovery across new and existing Android devices.

01

Open the ledger offline

After one successful online sign-in and access verification, eligible users can reopen the app without an internet connection. A first-time sign-in still requires internet access.

02

Visible trial countdown

Trial users can see the remaining time in hours, minutes, and seconds. The countdown updates without refreshing the entire ledger screen and automatically ends access when the trial expires.

03

Choose the right backup

Review up to five Google Drive backups by date, file size, and record counts. Empty or damaged backups are identified, and a new device cannot automatically upload an empty ledger.

Your records, your choice

Local first. Available offline after verification.

Core ledger information is stored on the user’s device. After the first online sign-in and access verification, eligible users can reopen the ledger offline. Google Drive access remains optional and is used only for app backup and restore.

App-specific Google Drive access

The app uses its private application-data folder and does not access ordinary Google Drive documents.

Protected backup workflow

New devices do not automatically upload an empty ledger. Empty or damaged backups are identified, and users choose a valid backup before restoring.

Trial and offline access

A visible countdown shows the remaining 24-hour trial time. Previously verified access can continue offline until the trial expires or the app reconnects and receives an updated account status.
